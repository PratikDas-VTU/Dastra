# Dastra User Guide

Welcome to Dastra, your offline productivity companion! 

## Installation

Dastra does not require an installer on Windows. Simply download the `.zip` file, extract it to a folder of your choice, and double-click `dastra.exe`.

On Android, download the `.apk` file and install it on your device.

## Core Features

### The Dashboard
The Dashboard is where you find all available tools. You can search for specific tools using the search bar, or filter them by category (e.g., Documents, Images, Security).

### The Workspace
The Workspace is your history. Every time you convert a PDF, compress an image, or generate a password, Dastra securely records the operation locally. You can easily reopen recent files or folders directly from this screen.

### Tools
- **PDF to Word**: Convert PDFs into editable `.docx` files.
- **Image Compression**: Reduce the file size of your photos without noticeable quality loss.
- **Password Generator**: Generate highly secure, random passwords entirely offline.

## Privacy Guarantee
Dastra operates 100% offline. We do not track your usage, we do not upload your files, and we do not require an internet connection to function. Your data remains entirely on your device.
